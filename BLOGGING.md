# Publishing blog posts

Blog entries are stored in `src/blogPosts.ts`.

To publish a new entry:

1. Add a new object at the beginning of the `blogPosts` array.
2. Give it a unique lowercase `slug`.
3. Add an exact publication date and category.
4. Write a short English title and optional Marathi title.
5. Keep the summary factual and concise.
6. Add a direct `sourceUrl` for factual or current-affairs claims.
7. Commit to `main`; GitHub Pages will redeploy automatically.

Do not upload photographs copied from social media. Use an official embed or
obtain written permission from the photographer.
